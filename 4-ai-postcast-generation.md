The major category you are missing that perfectly mirrors this exact 7-step lifecycle is AI Audio & Podcast Generation. [1, 2] 
Because audio is a time-based narrative medium like video, its lifecycle translates step-for-step:
## 1. Research
You research trending audio concepts, look up conversational topics, find target keywords, and scout popular guests or listener pain points.
## 2. Script
You write the podcast outline, host monologues, interview questions, or sponsorship ad reads.
## 3. Generation
Instead of hitting record in a studio, you feed your script into AI Voice Cloning & Audio Engines (like ElevenLabs, NotebookLM, or Suno for music). AI generates the highly realistic voiceover, sound effects, and background tracks. This also includes heavy AI editing, such as automated mastering, leveling, and removing background noise. [1, 2, 3] 
## 4. Distribution
The final audio file is distributed to hosting networks like Spotify for Podcasters or Apple Podcasts, automatically writing show notes and timestamps using text LLMs.
## 5. Comments Management
You track listener reviews on podcast platforms, reply to community forums (like Reddit threads or social clips built from the audio), and engage with listener call-ins.
## 6. Reporting
You aggregate metrics on downloads, listen-through rates (retention), listener geographic data, and platform distribution.
## 7. Analysis
You review where listeners skipped or dropped off (e.g., "Listeners dropped off heavily during the 3-minute mid-roll ad"). You take those insights to adjust the format, pacing, or topic selection, feeding straight back into step 1.
------------------------------
## 💡 Notable Mention: AI Code & App Generation
While not traditional marketing "content," building software or tools with AI follows a surprisingly identical pipeline:

   1. Research: Scoping user requirements and system architecture.
   2. Script: Writing technical documentation or user stories.
   3. Generation: Using AI (like Claude or GitHub Copilot) to generate code.
   4. Distribution: Deploying the app to a cloud platform or app store.
   5. Comments Management: Monitoring user bug reports and feature requests.
   6. Reporting: Checking server uptime, error logs, and active user metrics.
   7. Analysis: Reviewing where users get stuck in the app to plan the next software update. [4]
