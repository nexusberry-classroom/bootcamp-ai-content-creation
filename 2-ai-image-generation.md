Yes, the workflow is fundamentally the same for image generation, but it moves much faster because you are dealing with a static asset rather than a time-based medium.
The core phases translate perfectly, though the execution at each step is slightly different:
## 1. Pre-Production (The Blueprint)

* Research: Looking at competitor visuals, mood boarding, checking trending aesthetics, and identifying platform specifications (e.g., ad banner size vs. Instagram post).
* Script (Prompt Engineering): Instead of writing dialogue, this step involves writing highly detailed text prompts, negative prompts, aspect ratios, and style tags (e.g., lighting, camera lens types).

## 2. Production & Post-Production (The Execution)

* Generation: Running the prompt through AI image generators (like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion).
* Editing & Optimization (Crucial for Images): AI images frequently suffer from errors like distorted text, weird anatomy (like extra fingers), or blending artifacts. This step requires inpainting, outpainting, upscaling, overlaying graphic design elements, or jumping into Photoshop to clean up the details.

## 3. Post-Release (The Optimization Loop)

* Distribution: Publishing the image across websites, blogs, or social media with optimized copy, hashtags, or alt-text.
* Comments Management: Replying to community engagement, handling feedback, and answering design questions.
* Reporting & Analysis: Tracking metrics like click-through rates (CTR) for ads or engagement rates for social posts, then using those insights to refine your next prompt/concept.
